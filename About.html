Our Story

Clothing with a testimony, worn every day — not just on Sundays.

Who We Are

Redemption Clothing started with a simple conviction: faith shouldn't be boxed into Sunday best. We design streetwear that Christians can wear every day — bold enough for the street, rooted enough for the Word.

Every piece carries a Scripture reference stitched into the design, turning a T-shirt into a conversation and a hoodie into a testimony.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To equip believers with quality, stylish clothing that boldly represents their faith and sparks conversations about Christ in everyday spaces.

Vision

To become the leading Christian streetwear brand recognised for merging modern fashion with meaningful, faith-driven messaging.

Who We Serve

Christian teens and young adults who are active on social media, follow streetwear trends, and want faith-based alternatives to secular fashion brands.
